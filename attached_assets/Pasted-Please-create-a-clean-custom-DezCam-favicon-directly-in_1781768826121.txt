Please create a clean custom DezCam favicon directly inside this Replit project.

Goal:
Create and implement the DezCam “Option 8A” favicon.

This should be a favicon-native design, not a full logo. It needs to look clean, sharp, and readable in a browser tab like Gmail, Google Docs, Vercel, GitHub, and other professional web apps.

Design concept: Option 8A “Classic Split D”

* Dark circular background
* Minimal split-D symbol centered inside
* Left side of the D is a bold vertical green bar
* Right side of the D is a bold white/off-white curved D shape
* Together, the green bar and white curve should clearly read as a capital “D”
* It should be flat, clean, high-contrast, and not watered down

Brand colors:

* Background near-black: #0B1110
* Optional charcoal: #3D4240
* Primary DezCam green: #399A4B
* White/off-white: #F5F5F5 or #FFFFFF

Design requirements:

* Use a 512x512 SVG as the source/master file.
* The dark circle should fill most of the square canvas.
* The split-D mark should fill about 75–85% of the circle.
* The green vertical bar should be thick enough to remain visible at 16x16.
* The white D curve should be bold enough to remain readable at 16x16.
* Use simple geometric vector shapes.
* No text.
* No “DezCam” wordmark.
* No tagline.
* No “DC” monogram.
* No avatar icon.
* No gradients.
* No shadows.
* No glow.
* No texture.
* No thin strokes.
* No old favicon assets.

Suggested SVG geometry:

* Canvas/viewBox: 0 0 512 512
* Background circle:
    * cx: 256
    * cy: 256
    * r: about 230
    * fill: #0B1110
* Green vertical bar:
    * x: around 160
    * y: around 150
    * width: around 58–70
    * height: around 212–230
    * fill: #399A4B
    * slight corner radius is okay, but keep it simple
* White D curve:
    * Should sit to the right of the green bar
    * Should form the curved/right portion of a capital D
    * Use a bold, simple path or shape
    * Fill: #F5F5F5
    * The inside counter/open space must remain visible at small sizes
    * Avoid thin outlines or complicated curves

Important visual note:
The final icon should look close to the Option 8A concept: dark circle, green vertical split on the left, white D curve on the right. It should look bolder and cleaner than the current /dezcam-favicon.svg, which appears too watered down.
