Current issue:
- The favicon appears too small in the browser tab.
- The green vertical bar dominates, while the white D curve is too subtle.

Goal:
Create a stronger, bolder, more readable Option 8A split-D favicon.

Reference concept:
Option 8A should look like:
- Dark circular background
- Large split-D mark centered inside
- Thick green vertical bar on the left
- Large bold white curved D-shape on the right
- The overall mark should clearly read as a capital “D” even at 16x16 and 32x32

Important design corrections:
1. Make the split-D mark much larger.
   - The mark should fill about 85–90% of the dark circle’s usable space.
   - Reduce empty padding inside the circle.

2. Make the white D curve bolder and more visible.
   - The white curved shape should be at least as visually dominant as the green bar.
   - It should not look like a thin crescent.

3. Make the green bar wider and simpler.
   - Use a solid vertical rectangle or slightly rounded rectangle.
   - It should feel like the left stem of a capital D.

4. The final symbol should read as one simple “D.”
   - Green vertical stem + white curved right bowl.
   - Avoid making it look like two disconnected shapes.

5. Keep the design flat and crisp.
   - No gradients.
   - No shadows.
   - No glow.
   - No texture.
   - No thin outlines.
   - No small details.

Brand colors:
- Background circle: #0B1110
- Green bar: #399A4B
- White D curve: #F5F5F5 or #FFFFFF

Use an SVG path for the white D bowl, similar to a thick curved “)” shape with squared/clean ends, or use a compound path that creates the outer D curve and inner counter.
